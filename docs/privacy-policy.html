Razor City Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Razor City is a game published by Hollow Docks Games. This Privacy Policy explains how Razor City handles information when you use the game.

Information Razor City collects

Razor City does not require a user account and does not directly request your name, address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.

The game requires internet access for features that communicate with online services. Standard technical information such as IP address, device information, and network request information may be processed by service providers when the game communicates with their services.

Purchases and developer tips

Razor City may offer optional purchases or developer tips through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Hollow Docks Games does not receive or store your full payment-card information. Google may process transaction and device information according to its own privacy practices.

Advertising

Razor City may display advertising in versions that include an advertising service. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, ad interaction data, and other technical information as permitted by your device settings and applicable law. If advertising is enabled, Razor City will use advertising services subject to Google Play requirements and this policy will be updated as needed to identify material changes in data handling.

How information is used and shared

Information processed through third-party services is used to provide game functionality, process optional purchases, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain service security, and, where enabled, provide and measure advertising. Hollow Docks Games does not sell personal information.

Data security

Hollow Docks Games uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled by the game. Data transmitted to third-party services is handled using the security mechanisms provided by those services.

Data retention and deletion

Razor City does not currently provide user accounts or maintain a developer-operated database of personal user profiles. Game progress or preferences stored locally on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Information processed by Google Play or other third-party services is retained and deleted according to those providers' policies and applicable law.

Children

Razor City is not specifically directed to children. The game's declared target audience and any advertising configuration will follow the applicable Google Play requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Razor City's features, service providers, or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
